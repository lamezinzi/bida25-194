About Rove

Rove Crafts started from a love for making things by hand. Every bag is hand-crocheted by me, row by row, using sturdy t-shirt yarn. My goal is simple: to make cool, high-quality pieces that actually last. Rove is all about "slow fashion"—no mass production, just intentional work and a clean look that fits into your daily life.

Material & Sourcing

Every Rove piece is made with premium t-shirt yarn, a durable and sustainable textile that gives our bags their signature chunky but structure look. We exclusively use Elite T-shirt Yarns. Beyond the aesthetic, our materials are a win for the environment asit uses recycled textile fibers, reducing textile waste.
