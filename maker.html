Hi, I'm Lame Zinzi Pontsho

and I am the owner of Rove Crafts.I am also the hands behind every bag you see. Talk about one man one mission. I am a 19-year-old student studying Business Intelligence & Data Analytics at the Botswana School of Business Sciences.

How I Started Crocheting

While I’ve always wanted to learn to crochet, it wasn't until early 2025 that I finally picked up a hook. I am self-taught(with the aid of youtube of course).I skipped the basic practice rounds and dove straight into making a top within two days. I love a challenge, and that is the energy I bring to my craft.

What started as making a hibiscus-patterned tote for my grandmother quickly evolved into a business idea. I realized I wanted to create something unique, functional, and essential, that did not take too long to make. I made multiple bags with different stiches until i found tshirt yarn and a few months later Rove Crafts was born.
